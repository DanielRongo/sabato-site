Pricing FAQ

What buyers ask before they sign.

Anything else, ask on the call.

How is it billed?

Per AI token. One token is roughly a minute of conversation, sometimes a little more or less depending on the call. A ring nobody answers costs nothing. The call prices above are averages across our client calls, so a long call costs more and a short one less.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, and it is usually low. It covers the build and includes a starting package of tokens. The amount depends on the use case: custom integrations and bespoke workflows are quoted and billed separately.

Is there a minimum or a contract term?

You buy a package of tokens and have a set number of months to use them. We will agree the details with you on the call.

Can we run a pilot first?

Yes. You get a standard testing package of tokens to see whether Sabato is a fit on your real calls. A typical pilot runs 30 to 45 days, depending on how complex the workflow is.

How fast can we go live?

It depends on the use case, the implementation and how much customization you need. Typically from two weeks to 45 days from kickoff to the first live call.

Who runs the agent once it is live?

We do. Proactive human management, call reviews and fixes are part of the service.

Do other languages cost more?

No. Every language you sell in is included at the same price.

Does Sabato only work for e-commerce?

No. E-commerce is where we started, but Sabato fits any business with a high volume of inbound calls that still runs on phone lines and customer service operators.
