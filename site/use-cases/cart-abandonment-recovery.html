Example call

AGENT

Hi Marco, Sabato here calling for VeloStore. You left the CityRider e-bike in your cart earlier - do you have two minutes?

CALLER

Ah, yes. Honestly the delivery fee at the end put me off.

AGENT

Understood. Orders over six hundred euros ship free - you're forty short. The lock and light kit is thirty-five, which most CityRider buyers add anyway. That would make delivery free.

That's actually less than the delivery fee. Go on.

Done - I'm sending the updated cart to your WhatsApp now. The link holds your price for 48 hours.

Recover the cart, keep the reason

Every call ends with one of two outcomes: a recovered checkout, or the real reason it died - shipping cost, size doubt, payment friction - logged in plain text. Your email flow can't tell you why 97 in 100 stayed gone. A month of calls can.

Speed beats discounts

Recovery emails arrive hours later, competing in an inbox. A call reaches the buyer while the cart is still warm and the intent is still real - and it doesn't need to lead with a discount code to get attention. It leads with an answer.
