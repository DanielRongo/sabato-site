THE QUOTE QUEUE

A quote request is intent. A form is where it waits.

A buyer asking for a quote is the warmest lead you get - they've done the research, they have a budget, they're comparing two or three suppliers. Then they meet your quote form. Half the fields they can't answer from memory, so they submit it incomplete. Your team opens it days later, starts an email thread to fill the gaps, and by the time the number goes out, someone else's quote got there first. The intent didn't die. It just couldn't wait.

Example call

Live call - Sabato Agent · 02:41
CALLER

I need a quote for office chairs - we're fitting out a new floor.

AGENT

Happy to help. How many seats are we quoting?

CALLER

Forty, maybe forty-five.

AGENT

Noted as forty-five so the quote covers you. Fixed or adjustable arms, and do they need to stack for storage?

CALLER

Adjustable. No stacking. It needs to be delivered within three weeks, though.

AGENT

That works with two of our three lines. You'll have a structured quote for both options in your inbox within the hour - and if you'd rather talk it through, I can book a call with our sales team directly.
