Doubt is your biggest silent discount.

Seven out of ten carts never make it through checkout - the meta-average across 50 studies is a 70% abandonment rate. Behind a chunk of those is a question nobody answered in time: will it fit, will it work with what I own, which of these two is right for me. A buyer who can't resolve that doubt doesn't argue. They open a competitor's tab and leave. In a complex catalog, the answer usually exists - in your specs, your compatibility tables, your product data. It just can't speak.

Sell the second question

A buyer who asks one question usually has two. The second one - the size up, the longer runtime, the matching accessory - is where average order value moves. The agent answers from your catalog and recommends like your best floor salesperson would, without the script.
