THE WAITLIST PROBLEM

A waitlist is a queue of people you already convinced.

They found the product, chose it, and tried to buy it. The only thing that stopped them was you not having it. That is the warmest demand in your entire business - and the standard way of handling it is a templated email sent to everyone at once, landing in a promotions tab, hours or days after the shelf refilled. The people who wanted it most are the people most likely to have solved the problem elsewhere by then. Restock demand doesn't wait politely. It decays.

Illustrative - demand decay shown conceptually, not from measured data.

From restock to reserved

Who gets called first, how long a unit is held, and how many calls a restock triggers are your rules, set during onboarding. We build it on your stock data and run it for you.

Reach them before the inbox does

An email competes with forty others and waits for the customer to check it. A call arrives at the moment the stock does, and it can hold the unit while they think - something no notification email has ever been able to do.

The list also tells you the truth

Every 'no thanks, I bought it elsewhere' is data your restock report never captures: how long your waitlist actually holds, and which products lose customers to a stockout instead of just delaying them.
