THE WAITLIST PROBLEM

A waitlist is a queue of people you already convinced.

They found the product, chose it, and tried to buy it. The only thing that stopped them was you not having it. That is the warmest demand in your entire business — and the standard way of handling it is a templated email sent to everyone at once, landing in a promotions tab, hours or days after the shelf refilled. The people who wanted it most are the people most likely to have solved the problem elsewhere by then. Restock demand doesn't wait politely. It decays.

Illustrative — demand decay shown conceptually, not from measured data.
