AFTER THE CALL

Everything you agreed on, gone in four minutes.

A good call ends with a decision - this model, that spec, those two accessories. Then the buyer hangs up and the whole thing has to survive from memory. They can't recall which of the two variants you recommended. They can't find the product page again. The intent was real; the path back to checkout wasn't. A conversation that ends without a link is a conversation you have to have twice.

From conversation to checkout link

The summary is built from your live catalogue - real SKUs, real prices, a real checkout link. Shopify native; other platforms connected during onboarding.

The accessory nobody remembers to buy

The lock, the second battery, the matching part - agreed on the call, forgotten by the time the buyer reaches checkout. In the summary they're already in the cart, so the average order value the conversation earned is the one that actually completes.

A link beats a search bar

A buyer who has to re-find your product page is a buyer one search away from a competitor's. The summary lands while the conversation is still warm and takes exactly one tap to act on.
