Why this category phones

The call arrives after the money moved, which is why it costs more than it looks.

A fashion buyer will happily choose a jacket without speaking to anyone. The product page does its job. Then the parcel is late, or the 12 fitted like a 10, and the same buyer wants a human — quickly, and usually during a peak week when the queue is already at its worst.

This is deflection and retention work, not conversion work. We are honest about that: on a €40 t-shirt there is no call to win. On the returns and delivery queue behind it, there is a real cost to remove.

What the call sounds like

Inbound · 1 min 52 s · language: English · outcome: exchange booked, no refund

CALLER

Hi, I ordered a coat last week, it was meant to arrive Tuesday and the tracking hasn’t moved since Friday.

AGENT

Let me look. Order ending 8213, the coat in navy — it’s at the Milan depot and it was held there on Saturday because of a failed delivery attempt at the address. It’s scheduled to go out again tomorrow morning.

CALLER

Nobody came on Saturday.

AGENT

That happens more than it should. I can either leave it on tomorrow’s round, or reroute it to a pickup point near you — there’s one about four hundred metres from the delivery address.

CALLER

The pickup point. And actually — if the size is wrong when it turns up, how does that work?

AGENT

Thirty days from delivery. If it’s the size rather than the coat, I’d do an exchange instead of a return: the navy is in stock in both the size above and below, so it ships the day we scan yours back.

CALLER

Good. Do the pickup point then.

AGENT

Rerouted, and I’ll text you the pickup code when it arrives. Anything else on this one?

What the agent has to read

The data reality behind a fashion answer

This category asks less of your product data and more of your operational data. The failure mode is not a wrong recommendation — it is a confident wrong delivery date, which turns one annoyed customer into two calls.

THE HONEST PART

Bad source data produces confident wrong answers. If a field is stale, the agent will speak it fluently — and a wrong answer at speed is worse than a slow right one, because it converts into an order that fails. We audit the fields this page depends on before the pilot, and we cap the agent to hand over on anything unmapped.

Carrier tracking events

Raw events, not a status label, so an exception can be explained rather than recited.

Order promise date

Returns window and policy

Per channel and per promotion, so the answer on the phone matches the portal.

Variant availability

Live, so an exchange can be offered instead of a refund while the buyer is on the line.

Inbound purchase orders

Common questions

Do fashion customers actually call?

Not usually before buying — that is the honest answer, and it is why this page leads with post-purchase. They call when a parcel is late, when a size is wrong, or when a refund has not landed. In peak weeks that queue is the one that overflows.

Is voice AI worth it if our average order value is low?

It depends on your call volume, not your order value. If the post-purchase queue is large and seasonal, the case is deflection and retention. If it is small and steady, self-service is probably enough and we will say so on the intro call.

Can the agent handle a size or fit question?

Only where you hold garment measurements per size. Where you do not, it hands over rather than guessing — fit is the one question in this category where a confident wrong answer converts directly into a return.

What happens during a sale week when volume triples?

Concurrency is the point. The agent takes every call at once rather than queueing them, which is when abandoned calls in fashion usually spike — and abandoned calls skew towards the customers with a problem.

Does it work in more than one language?

Yes, and in fashion that matters more than in most categories, because cross-border share is high. The agent detects the caller’s language on the first turn and stays in it, including the follow-up message.
