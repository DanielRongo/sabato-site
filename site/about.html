About Sabato

About Sabato

Rebuilding the economics
of e-commerce

E-commerce has optimized every click in the funnel, except the conversation.

We build the AI layer that resolves, retains, and sells in every customer interaction, turning your busiest cost center into your most efficient growth engine.

Where commerce gets its margin back

After years building and scaling products at Google, Meta, and King, we kept seeing the same blind spot: brands pour millions into acquisition, then treat the moment a customer actually reaches out as a cost to minimize. That's backwards. The conversation is where loyalty, average order value, and lifetime value are won or lost. Sabato is the AI layer that makes that moment efficient - resolving instantly, recovering sales in real time, and turning support from a line item into a growth lever. This is what the next decade of e-commerce runs on.

What's Sabato AI

Sabato is a managed voice AI platform built for e-commerce. It plugs into your store and handles calls across the entire customer journey - pre-sales consultation, cart abandonment recovery, order tracking, complaints, quoting, and more. Pre-configured workflows, connected to your product catalog, fully managed by our team. Live in two weeks.
